Got questions about admissions, batches, or placements? We have answers.

What are the batch timings for offline and online classes?
We offer flexible morning (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM), afternoon (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM), and evening (6:30 PM - 9:00 PM) batches for both online live classes and offline classroom sessions in Pune.
Do non-IT or non-CS students get placement support?
Yes! Over 40% of our successful alumni come from Mechanical, ENTC, Civil, and Commerce backgrounds. Our foundational modules start from scratch and equip you with solid coding skills.
How does the Mock Test and Placement module work?
Our interactive Mock Test module provides real exam simulation with instant evaluation, score breakdown, and answer reviews. Students eligible for placement drives must pass subject benchmark tests.
Can I pay course fees in installments?
Yes, we offer flexible no-cost EMI and installment options to ensure financial ease for all students.
